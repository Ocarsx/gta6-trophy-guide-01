Roadmap to the Platinum

No difficulty trophy, and nothing is permanently missable after the story if you keep a save before the final mission — but the partner trophies are far easier while your partner is available. Follow the phases in order and you will not have to replay the story.

  1. Phase 1 — Story first (Chapters 1–4)

    Play the story straight through. All 18 story trophies unlock naturally, including the four chapter trophies and the final-mission gold. While you play, do the missable-flagged partner trophies (Hot Together, Retail Therapy) as soon as your partner is beside you, and sample each of the 9 activity types for Master of None.

  2. Phase 2 — Contacts & strangers

    Finish the mission chains for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, plus 8 Strangers & Freaks strands. Check the map for their icons after every chapter — new missions unlock between chapters.

  3. Phase 3 — The big map sweep

    One systematic sweep of Leonida covers four trophies at once: explore all 52 areas, WAiNK It at 100 locations, meditate at all 18 spots, and hunt Collectible Cards region by region. Register vehicles in the Scooter Bros app as you find them.

  4. Phase 4 — Grinds & clean-up

    Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win 20 different races and the 14 drone races, pull off 250 stunts, rob 5 Bank Vaults, and finish the 50 challenges. Then check the in-game completion screen and mop up whatever 100% still needs.

  5. Phase 5 — Platinum

    Earn the last remaining trophy and Vice Got a Price! pops automatically. Estimated total: 80+ hours for a first-time completionist.
